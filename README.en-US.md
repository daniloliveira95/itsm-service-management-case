# ITSM Service Management Case

> An anonymized portfolio case demonstrating the planning, implementation and evolution of an IT Service Management (ITSM) environment using Jira Service Management.

## 📌 Overview

This case represents a real professional experience in the implementation and evolution of an IT Service Management environment.

The project involved the transition from an existing Service Desk platform to Jira Service Management, combining requirements gathering, process analysis, workflow design, SLA definition, data migration, user training and operational monitoring.

All company names, internal information and sensitive operational details have been intentionally omitted or generalized.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The main objective was to implement a more structured and scalable ITSM environment capable of supporting service operations, improving request management, increasing process visibility and providing operational indicators for decision-making.

The project also aimed to facilitate the transition from the previous Service Desk platform while maintaining operational continuity.

---

## 🧩 Business Context

The IT operation required a more structured approach to service management, with greater visibility into:

- Incident and request management
- Service workflows
- SLA monitoring
- Backlog management
- Recurring incidents
- Operational performance
- User access and responsibilities
- Management reporting

The existing environment was replaced by Jira Service Management as part of the ITSM modernization initiative.

---

## 🔎 Requirements Analysis

The implementation started with understanding the needs of the IT operation and its users.

Activities included:

- Gathering requirements from users and operational teams
- Understanding existing service processes
- Mapping the current Service Desk workflow
- Identifying opportunities for process improvement
- Defining categories and service flows
- Establishing SLA requirements
- Translating operational needs into Jira configurations
- Validating the proposed solution with users

The objective was not simply to configure a tool, but to make the platform reflect the actual service management process.

---

## 🛠️ Solution

Jira Service Management was implemented as the primary ITSM platform.

The solution included the structuring of:

- Service request workflows
- Incident management flows
- SLA policies
- Ticket categorization
- Operational queues
- Internal service processes
- Access and permission controls
- Reporting and operational indicators

The implementation also included the migration from the previous OTRS-based Service Desk environment to Jira Service Management.

---

## 🔄 Migration

The project included the transition:

`OTRS → Jira Service Management`

The migration required attention to:

1. Existing service processes
2. Ticket and operational data
3. User requirements
4. New workflow structures
5. SLA definitions
6. Operational continuity
7. User adoption

The migration was treated as a process transition rather than simply a technical platform replacement.

---

## 👥 User Adoption

A major component of the implementation was user adoption.

More than **300 users** were trained on the new platform.

Training activities focused on:

- Understanding the new Service Desk
- Correct ticket registration
- New workflows
- Service request processes
- User responsibilities
- Operational continuity during the transition

The goal was to ensure that the new platform could be effectively adopted by the organization.

---

## 📊 Operational Indicators

The IT operation established indicators to monitor service performance and support management decisions.

Examples included:

- SLA
- TMA
- Backlog
- Reincidence
- Ticket volume

These indicators were used in operational analysis and management reporting.

---

## 🔐 Access Management

The project also considered access governance and the principle of least privilege.

Access controls were structured according to operational responsibilities, reducing unnecessary permissions and supporting a more controlled IT environment.
